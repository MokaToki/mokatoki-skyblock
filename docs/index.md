## 🚀 Beginner Quick-Start Guide

If you're new to the server, follow these four simple steps to establish your skyblock empire:

### 1. Create Your Island
Start your adventure by creating a new island.
```bash
/is create
```
Choose a starting schematic, and you'll be teleported to your new home.

### 2. Level Up & Earn Starting Funds
Open your skills menu and check your active quests to start earning money and unlocking stat boosts.

* Run `/skills` to check your leveling progress.
* Run `/quests` to view your main quests.

### 3. Automate Resource Collection
Save up your earnings to buy automation assistants.

* Purchase a minion from the `/minion store`.
* Place it on your island and link it to a nearby chest to automatically farm and store resources.

### 4. Climb the Leaderboard
Boost your island level to secure a spot at the top of the server.

* Deposit money into the shared island bank via `/is bank`.
* Place and stack valuable blocks (like Diamond, Gold, and Netherite) on your island.
* Check the current rankings using `/is top`.
