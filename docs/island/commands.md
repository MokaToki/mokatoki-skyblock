# 🏝️ Island Commands & Setup

Managing your island revolves around the primary `/is` command interface. Below is a comprehensive reference guide for all commands, grouped by function.

---

## 🏝️ Core Island Commands

These commands allow you to create, navigate, and manage the general properties of your island.

| Command | Alternative | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `/is` | `/is menu` | Opens your main Skyblock control GUI. |
| `/is create` | - | Creates a brand-new Skyblock island. |
| `/is home` | `/is tp` | Teleports you safely back to your island spawn point. |
| `/is setteleport` | - | Sets a custom spawn/teleport location for your island. |
| `/is level` | - | Recalculates and displays your island's current Level and Worth. |
| `/is settings` | - | Opens settings to toggle PvP, mob spawning, and visitor permissions. |
| `/is border` | - | Customizes or toggles your personal island world border. |

---

## 🏦 Island Bank & Upgrades

Pool your resources with team members to fund upgrades and expand your island's limits.

| Command | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| `/is bank` | Deposit or withdraw money from your shared island bank to fund island growth. |
| `/is upgrade` | Opens the upgrade menu! Spend money to increase crop growth speeds, spawner spawn rates, hopper limits, max team members, and border size. |

---

## 👥 Team & Visitor Commands

Control who can access, build, or visit your island. Use coop commands to collaborate temporarily.

| Command | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| `/is invite <player>` | Invites a player to join your island team. |
| `/is kick <player>` | Removes a member from your island team. |
| `/is leave` | Leave your current island team. |
| `/is coop <player>` | Temporarily grants building/access rights to a visitor. |
| `/is uncoop <player>` | Revokes temporary coop permissions from a visitor. |
| `/is ban <player>` | Bans a troublesome player from visiting your island. |
| `/is unban <player>` | Unbans a player from visiting your island. |

---

## 🌐 Warps & Leaderboards

Discover other players' creations or share your own with the community.

| Command | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| `/is top` | Opens the island leaderboard sorted by total Worth & Level. |
| `/is warp <player>` | Teleports to another player's public Welcome/Warp sign. |
| `/is setwarp <name>` | Sets up a public warp point for visitors on your island. |
| `/is delwarp <name>` | Removes a public warp point from your island. |
