# 📈 Island Level & Worth System

Your position on the server-wide **`/is top`** leaderboard is determined by the total **Worth** and **Level** of your island. This rating is calculated dynamically based on the blocks placed within your island boundaries.

---

## 💎 Valuable Blocks

Placing blocks of precious metals and gems is the primary way to raise your island level. The higher the tier of the block, the more worth points it contributes.

The following blocks increase your island value:
* 🪙 **Iron Blocks** — Entry-level progression blocks.
* 🪙 **Gold Blocks** — Moderate-tier progression blocks.
* 💎 **Diamond Blocks** — High-tier progression blocks.
* 💚 **Emerald Blocks** — Advanced progression blocks.
* 🌌 **Netherite Blocks** — Premium/highest-tier progression blocks.

---

## 📦 Stacked Blocks

As your island grows, placing individual blocks can take up valuable building space. To solve this, you can stack valuable blocks in a single block space.

* **Command:** `/is toggle blocks`
* **How it works:** Toggling this setting allows you to stack blocks of the same type directly on top of each other. This consolidates their worth and saves physical space, allowing you to maximize your island score without filling your entire island with block grids.
