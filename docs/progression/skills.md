# 🌟 Skills System

Level up your character natively by performing standard in-game actions! The skills system adds RPG-style character development that rewards your dedication with permanent stat upgrades and special abilities.

---

## 📜 Skill Commands

| Command | Alternative | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `/skills` | `/sk` | Opens the main Skills menu displaying levels, XP progress, and active stat bonuses. |
| `/skills top` | - | Opens leaderboards showing the top players for each skill. |

---

## 📊 Character Stats

As you level up your skills, you will receive permanent bonuses to your core statistics:

* ❤️ **Health:** Increases your maximum health pool.
* 💪 **Strength:** Boosts your physical damage output.
* 🛡️ **Defense:** Reduces incoming damage from mobs and players.
* 🍀 **Luck:** Increases the chance of double or rare drops from farming and mining.

---

## ⚙️ How Skills Work

You earn experience (XP) in each skill by performing relevant tasks. Here are the core skills:

| Skill | How to Train | Main Benefits |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mining** | Mine stone, ores, and generators | High double-drop chances, defense bonuses |
| **Farming** | Harvest crops and replant | Double crop rates, health bonuses |
| **Combat** | Defeat hostile mobs and entities | Damage multipliers, strength bonuses |
| **Foraging** | Chop down trees and clear logs | Double log drops, strength/health |
| **Fishing** | Catch fish and treasure in water | Rare loot tables, luck bonuses |
| **Alchemy** | Brew custom and vanilla potions | Extended potion duration |
| **Enchanting** | Use enchantment tables and books | XP multipliers, luck bonuses |
| **Archery** | Shoot targets and mobs with bows | Ranged damage multipliers |
| **Defense** | Take damage from mobs and players | Permanent damage reduction |
| **Excavation** | Dig dirt, sand, gravel, and clay | Treasure drops, luck bonuses |
| **Agility** | Run, sprint, and jump | Speed boosts, fall damage reduction |

!!! tip "Perks and Abilities"
    Each skill also contains active abilities (triggered by right-clicking with the skill's tool and hitting a block/mob) and passive perks that unlock at milestone levels. Keep leveling to unlock their full potential!
