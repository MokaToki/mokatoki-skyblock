# 📜 Quests & Jobs

Supplement your island's economy by taking up jobs and completing quests. Doing so awards in-game money, keys, and special materials!

---

## 📜 Quests

Quests provide objectives that you can complete during gameplay:

* **Command:** `/quests` or `/q`
* **Main Quests:** Long-term milestones representing your overarching progress on the server.

---

## 💼 Jobs

Jobs reward you with passive cash and experience as you perform actions like mining, farming, and hunting. 

### Job Commands
* `/jobs` - Opens the main Jobs GUI to browse, join, or leave jobs.
* `/jobs stats` - Checks your active job levels, current experience, and total daily earnings.

### Available Jobs
You can join the following professions to match your island activities:

| Job | Action Rewarded | Description / Ideal For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Blacksmith** | Repairing and upgrading gear / items | Tool upkeep and weapon forging |
| **Brewer** | Brewing potions and custom elixirs | Potion brewers and alchemists |
| **Builder** | Placing blocks and constructing structures | Building island expansions |
| **Crafter** | Crafting items and materials | General crafting and production |
| **Digger** | Excavating dirt, sand, gravel, and clay | Terraforming and block clearing |
| **Explorer** | Traveling and discovering zones | Exploring and map traversal |
| **Farmer** | Harvesting crops and planting seeds | Large-scale agricultural farms |
| **Fisher** | Catching fish and treasure from water | Custom fishing ponds |
| **Hunter** | Defeating hostile mobs and animals | Mob grinding arenas |
| **Lumberjack** | Chopping down trees and clearing logs | Tree farms and woodcutting |
| **Miner** | Mining stone, cobble, and various ores | Cobble and ore generators |
| **Shepheard** | Shearing sheep and breeding animals | Livestock farming and shearing |
