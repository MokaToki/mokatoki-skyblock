# 🐾 Pets System

Unlock loyal companions that follow you around, show off custom cosmetic particles, and grant significant gameplay buffs. Pets are dynamic entities that grow stronger as you train.

---

## 📜 Pet Commands

| Command | Alternative | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `/pets` | `/pet` | Opens your Pet collection GUI to summon, dismiss, or manage pets. |
| `/pet menu` | - | Accesses the configuration menu for pet leveling, details, and active skills. |

---

## ⚙️ How Pets Work

Pets act as passive stat multipliers and companions. Summon a pet from your collection menu, and it will float alongside you.

### 📈 Leveling Your Pet
Your pets gain experience points (XP) as you perform activities corresponding to their specialty:

* ⛏️ **Mining Pets** — Train them by mining stone, ores, or custom cobble generators.
* 🧑‍🌾 **Farming Pets** — Train them by planting seeds and harvesting mature crops.
* ⚔️ **Combat Pets** — Train them by fighting monsters and hostile entities.

As your pet levels up, its passive buffs scale dynamically, becoming significantly more powerful.

### ⚔️ Passive Multipliers & Buffs
Summoning a pet activates specific passive multipliers:

* 📈 **Extra XP Gains** — Earn bonus experience points toward your skills and jobs.
* 🌾 **Double Crop Harvests** — Increase your crop yield multipliers automatically while farming.
* 💥 **Bonus Damage** — Deal extra combat damage to clear mob grinding spawners faster.
