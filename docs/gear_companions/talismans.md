# 🧿 Talismans

Equip passive artifacts that grant unique power boosts, combat stats, and utility modifications. Talismans are essential tools for optimizing your character's combat, speed, and farming efficiency.

---

## 📜 Talisman Commands

| Command | Alternative | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `/talismans bag` | `/talisman bag` | Opens your active Talisman Bag. |

---

## ⚙️ How Talismans Work

To gain the benefits of a talisman, you must carry it in either your character's main inventory or, preferably, inside your dedicated Talisman Bag:

* **Talisman Bag:** Open this bag via `/talismans bag`. Storing your talismans here keeps them active without cluttering your inventory slots!
* **Unique Effects:** Each talisman provides a unique perk. Examples of passive buffs include:
    * ⚡ **Speed:** Increases your base walking/sprinting speed.
    * 👁️ **Night Vision:** Grants permanent night vision.
    * 🛡️ **Resistance:** Provides permanent damage mitigation.
    * 💥 **Critical Hit Chance:** Increases your chance to deal critical hits in combat.
    * 💰 **Increased Loot:** Boosts the amount of mob drops or money earned.

!!! warning "Duplicate Talismans"
    Having duplicate talismans of the same type in your bag or inventory does **not** stack their effects. Only the highest-tier version of a duplicate talisman will apply!
