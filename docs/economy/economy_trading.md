# 🏪 Economy, Trades & Auctions

Mokatoki Skyblock runs on a robust virtual economy. Acquire wealth, buy resources from the official shop, list your items on the Global Auction House, or trade securely with other players.

---

## 📜 Economy Commands

Manage your wallet and perform basic money transfers:

| Command | Alternative | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `/balance` | `/bal` | Displays your current wallet balance. |
| `/pay <player> <amount>` | - | Safely sends money directly to another online player's wallet. |
| `/shop` | - | Opens the main GUI server shop to buy/sell blocks, minerals, crops, spawners, and farm essentials. |

---

## 🛍️ Auction House (`/ah`)

The Auction House allows players to sell items globally to the highest bidder or as a direct buy-it-now listing.

* **`/ah`** - Opens the global marketplace interface where you can browse and purchase items listed by other players.
* **`/ah sell <price>`** - Lists the item currently in your main hand for sale at the specified price. 

---

## 🤝 Player Trading (`/trade`)

To prevent scams and ensure safe item-for-item or item-for-money transfers, always use the secure trading system:

* **Command:** `/trade <player>`
* **Mechanism:** Sends an interactive trade invitation to another online player. Once accepted, a split-screen inventory menu will open where both players can safely offer items and money. Both parties must click the confirmation lock to complete the trade.
