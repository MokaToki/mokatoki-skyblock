# ⚔️ Special Events

Compete against the entire server in scheduled mini-games, competitions, and races! Server-wide events are scheduled challenges that reward the highest-performing players with exclusive loot, items, in-game currency, and cosmetic crate keys.

---

## 📜 Event Commands

Keep track of upcoming challenges and schedules:

* **Command:** `/events`
* **Function:** Opens a calendar/schedule GUI displaying active and upcoming events, timers, and reward brackets.

---

## ⚙️ How Events Work

Events trigger automatically throughout the day, prompting players to participate in themed server-wide activities (such as fishing competitions, block mining rushes, or mob hunting contests). 

### What You Can Win
Competing in these events allows you to earn valuable items to speed up your progression, including:

* 🔑 **Crate Keys:** Used at spawn to unlock cosmetic and utility crates.
* 🪙 **In-Game Currency:** Direct boosts to your wallet balance.
* 📦 **Unique Resources:** Exclusive tools, custom weapons, advanced spawners, and boosters.
