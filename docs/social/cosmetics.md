# 💄 Cosmetics

Personalize your appearance and stand out in the chat and spawn! Our cosmetic items are 100% visual and do not grant any unfair gameplay advantages, aligning with our fair-play values.

---

## 📜 Cosmetic Commands

Manage your active cosmetics with these commands:

| Command | Alternative | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `/cosmetics` | `/uc` | Opens the main Cosmetics Selection GUI. |
| `/cosmetics clear` | - | Instantly disables all currently active cosmetic effects on your character. |

---

## 🎭 Cosmetic Types

You can unlock and equip a variety of cosmetic modifiers:

* ✨ **Particle Trails:** Beautiful particle patterns (e.g. hearts, flame trails, notes) that follow you as you walk, jump, and fly.
* 👒 **Hats:** Custom hats and blocks displayed on your head.
* 👔 **Suits:** Colorful armor combinations that trigger unique animations when a full set is equipped.
* 💬 **Emotes:** Quick visual chat expressions and reactions.
* 🐕 **Cosmetic Pets:** Mini-companions that accompany you for display only (note: these do not offer the leveling stat multipliers of [Pets](../gear_companions/pets.md)).
